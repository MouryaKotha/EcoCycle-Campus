How It Works

A seamless three-step process to dispose of your e-waste responsibly while earning points.

1. Scan

Locate the nearest campus smart kiosk and scan its QR code using the EcoCycle web app.

2. Deposit

The bin unlocks automatically. Deposit your approved e-waste securely.

3. Earn

Get points instantly applied to your account, redeemable for cafeteria discounts.

Platform Features

Secure Kiosk Access

Our smart kiosks remain locked until authenticated via your student profile, ensuring a secure chain of custody for all deposited devices.

Deposit Verification

Each transaction is securely logged with a unique digital receipt, preventing double-spending and ensuring accuracy.

Cafeteria Rewards

Your sustainability efforts directly subsidize your meals. Redeem points for exclusive vouchers valid across campus dining.

Impact Tracking

Monitor your personal carbon footprint reduction and see the collective impact of your campus community in real-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of e-waste is accepted?

We accept cables, earphones, chargers, power banks, and old mobile phones. Please ensure no swollen batteries are deposited for safety reasons.

How do I use my cafeteria vouchers?

Once you redeem points for a voucher, a digital QR code and ID is generated in your transaction history. Show this to the cafeteria cashier.
